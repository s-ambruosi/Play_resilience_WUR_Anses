# Play_resilience_WUR_Anses
Data and code for the manuscript Associations between play behaviour and physiological responses to immune and social challenges in pigs across experimental conditions submitted to _animal: The international journal of animal biosciences_
# Project description
This project contributes to the interaction between farm animal behaviour and resilience, which is now considered essential for positive animal welfare. For this project, we combined the data from two different institutes to investigate potential associations between piglets’ play behaviour pre-challenge and subsequent resilience to several challenges (LPS injection, frustration, and enterotoxigenic E. coli inoculation).
The results suggest a consitent negative association between the amount of play and the amplitude of physiological stress responses to the challenges. Our findings thus encourage future studies to consider play behaviour to provide valuable insights into farm animals’ abilities to cope with challenges, beyond its traditional interpretation as an indicator of positive affective states.
